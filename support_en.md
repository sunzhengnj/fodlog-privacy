# FoodLog - Help & Support

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How to use AI to identify food?
Tap the camera button on the home page, take a photo of your food, and the AI will automatically identify the food and calculate its calories.

### How to enable iCloud sync?
Go to "Profile" -> "Settings" -> "iCloud Sync". Once enabled, your data will automatically sync across all your devices.

### How to restore purchases?
Go to "Profile" -> "Premium Center", and tap the "Restore Purchase" button.

### Will I lose my data?
With iCloud sync enabled, your data is securely stored in your itCloud account. Reinstalling the app will not cause data loss.
