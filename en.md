# Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 27, 2026

Welcome to **Folog**. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

## 1. Data We Collect

### Information You Provide
- Account info: Apple ID sign-in identifier
- Profile: Nickname, avatar, daily calorie goal
- Diet records: Food names, calories, macronutrients
- Health data: Step count and active energy (with your permission)

### Automatically Collected
- Device info: Device model, OS version
- Usage data: Feature usage patterns

## 2. How We Use Your Data
- Provide food logging and nutrition analysis
- Generate personalized diet reports
- AI food recognition and smart suggestions
- Multi-device data sync
- Improve our products and services

## 3. Data Storage
- Your data is stored in your Apple iCloud account
- We do not store your personal data on our servers
- All data transfers use HTTPS encryption

## 4. HealthKit Data
We may request access to Apple Health data including active energy, step count, and weight. This data is **never** uploaded to third-party servers or shared with advertisers.

## 5. AI Services
When using AI features, your input is sent to AI providers for processing. This data is used solely for analysis, not stored permanently, and not used to train models.

## 6. Third-Party Services
- **Apple CloudKit**: Data storage and sync
- **Apple HealthKit**: Health data integration
- **StoreKit**: Subscription management
- **FatSecret**: Food nutrition database

## 7. Data Sharing
We do not sell or share your personal information unless required by law or with your consent.

## 8. Children's Privacy
The App is not intended for children under 13.

## 9. Your Rights
- Access, correct, and delete your data
- Withdraw permissions
- Delete your account via App settings
